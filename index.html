Catherine Tole

Executive, Group & Team Coach · ICF PCC

Catherine is a PCC-certified executive, group, and team coach who works with leaders across different regions of the world. She partners primarily with mid- and senior-level organisational leaders, helping them strengthen their leadership presence, navigate transitions, and lead with greater awareness and impact.

Catherine has a particular passion for working with women leaders who find themselves at a turning point in their journey — women who have achieved much, yet are asking deeper questions about meaning, legacy, and what the next chapter of leadership looks like. Through coaching, she creates a reflective space that enables leaders to gain clarity, challenge limiting beliefs, and step into their next level of growth.
